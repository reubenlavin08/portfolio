# PRODUCT.md

**Surface:** reubenlavin.me — personal portfolio, single page.
**register:** brand

## Users

1. **UBC engineering-transfer reviewers and professors.** Skimming for evidence that the applicant builds real things. Bright screen, mid-morning, under two minutes of attention.
2. **Hiring engineers at small hardware shops** (Sentry Devices, Brivia). Looking for one thing: does this person ship working firmware and CV pipelines, or just talk about them.
3. **Mentors and peers** sent the link directly.

The page has to answer "what has he actually built, and does it work" before it answers anything else.

## Brand voice

Three words: **exact, warm, unshowy.**

Reuben's own writing rule: simple, matter-of-fact, elegant. No hype adjectives. A measured number beats an adjective every time ("119.58° × 63.12° measured FOV" beats "wide-angle"). Where AI helped, one plain sentence inside the project, never a standalone claim.

## Reference

Mercury (mercury.com) for the *system*: warm-white ground, zero shadows, sub-pixel hairlines, a two-family variable-sans hierarchy at light display weights, one committed indigo, a 4px radius grid, and a dark footer that closes the page.

Bauhaus / 1960s Danish modern for the *stance*: form follows function. Structure is visible and does work — numbered sections, a strict grid, hairlines instead of decoration, color that encodes meaning rather than mood. Warmth comes from the paper and the wood-toned neutrals, not from ornament.

## Anti-references

- The dark terminal-green developer portfolio (what this site was). Reads as costume.
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, drop shadows, glow.
- Editorial-magazine pastiche: display-serif italic headlines, drop caps, broadsheet columns. Wrong register for a hardware portfolio.
- Futura pastiche as shorthand for "Bauhaus."
- Uppercase tracked eyebrow labels stacked above every single section.
- Hero-metric templates. He has real numbers; they belong next to the thing they measure.

## Strategic principles

1. **Evidence over assertion.** Every project shows the artifact: video, still, live playground.
2. **Skimmable in 45 seconds, deep if you stay.** The case-study skim layer is load-bearing.
3. **Color is functional.** Indigo means interactive. Vermilion means live/in-progress. Nothing is colored for decoration.
4. **Nothing that only looks good.** If a treatment does not help a reader find or judge something, it comes out.
